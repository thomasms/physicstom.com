After recently reading, or I should instead say, consuming, Hofstadters "I Am a Strange Loop" book, it took a while for me to digest it and actually process the ideas that 
he was trying to convey. Not only do I love his writing style but the ideas presented were so fresh that it was really hard to put the book down. This was really one of the 
only books that actually tries to address the "hard problem" of consciousness, and he does this by removing this notion of the bird in the bird cage, or the pilot in the 
cockpit. There is simply no bird, no pilot, no commander, or whatever you like to think of yourself, it is just a hallucination manifesting as such. This idea is refreshing 
and I find it actually quite comforting.

- consciousness is a scalar quantity, not a binary one, and everyone has a different level of it
- the self is an hallucination, that emerges from a system trying to deal with complexity by forming abstractions, one of which is the self
